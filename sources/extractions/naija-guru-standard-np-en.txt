How to write Nigerian Pidgin — web extract (Naija Guru Standard NP)
Source: https://naija.guru/en/grammar/how-to-write-nigerian-pidgin/
Standard NP (2024) — engineered for English-keyboard adoption and tooling.

KEY PRINCIPLES:
- Latin alphabet; English keyboard friendly
- Minimal tone marking: acute on "tone words" only; low tone unmarked
- Examples: dey gym vs déy gym; go vs gó
- Most English loanwords spelled same as English; exceptions in Naija Guru dictionary
- Standard English punctuation
- Plural dem: hyphenated (man-dem, people-dem, army-dem)
- Reduplicated words: hyphenated (sharp-sharp, small-small, wuru-wuru)
- Some compound nouns hyphenated (i-too-know, ghana-must-go, bend-down-select)

CONSONANTS documented with IPA (includes gb, kp, ch, sh, j, ny, zh in examples).
