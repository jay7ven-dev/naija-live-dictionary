GUIDE TO STANDARD NAIJÁ ORTHOGRAPHY — web extract (IFRA Nigeria)
Source: https://ifra-nigeria.org/research/former-projects/89-guide-to-standard-naija-orthography-an-nla-harmonized-writing-system-for-common-naija-publications
Authors: Christine I. Ofulue & David O. Esizimetor (2010)

KEY PRINCIPLES (SNO):
- Phonetically based; common core Naijá (not one regional dialect)
- Digraphs/diacritics: ch, gb, sh, kp, zh
- Minimal tone marking: high tone acute only; low unmarked
- 28 letters: a b ch d e f g gb h i j k kp l m n o p r s sh t u v w y z zh
- e merges [e]/[ε]; o merges [o]/[ɔ] for typing convenience
- Affixation: single word (woka, bois)
- Compounds (modifier): hyphenated (strong-hed, akara-wuman)
- Reduplication (bound): together (potopoto, chukuchuku)
- Intensification (free morpheme): separate words (wel wel, bad bad)
- Plural dem: hyphenated (pikin-dem); English-origin plurals may stay as one word (bois)
- Emphatic possessive: hyphenate -im (Meri-im mama)

TONE EXAMPLES:
- baba (father) vs bába (barber)
- de (PROG) vs dé (is/are, stay)
- Naija (Niger) vs Naijá (Nigeria / the language)
