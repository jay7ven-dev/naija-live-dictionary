This project follows the IFRA/NLA Standard Naijá Orthography (SNO) for every headword. We are not affiliated with IFRA Nigeria.

What SNO means here

  • Latin script with digraphs such as ch, gb, kp, sh, zh.
  • Minimal tone marking: acute accent only when needed to tell words apart.
  • English loanwords often adapted to Naijá sound patterns (e.g. buk not book as the headword).
  • Bound reduplication written as one word (e.g. shapshap); social hyphen forms can be variants.

Other writing systems

Community standards such as Naija Guru Standard NP overlap with SNO in many places but differ on hyphenation and some English loans. In this dictionary those forms are stored as variants, not competing headwords.

What we do not publish

We cite the IFRA guide as authority. We do not redistribute the full IFRA PDF or long verbatim guide text without permission. See project licensing policy (Decision D4).
