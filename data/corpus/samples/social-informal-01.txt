I dey go market wit my pickin dem, abeg make we sharp sharp go before rain start.
Dem pikin no fit chop becos food no dey for house.
I wan buy book and pen for skul tumoro.
My broda say e go drive moto go Lagos — na big wahala on top road.
Pickin dem de play for yard; mama de cook moimoi.
You sabi say Naija Pidgin sweet? Make we talk am well well.
